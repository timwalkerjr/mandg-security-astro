Welcome to the M&G Security news and resources hub. This is where we'll share
practical security guidance, event-safety planning tips, and updates about the
work our team is doing across Berks County, Pennsylvania.

M&G Security was founded in 1997 by former law enforcement officers, and our
staff brings over 60 years of combined law enforcement experience to every
assignment — from sporting events and festivals to construction sites and
overnight restaurant coverage. In the articles ahead, we'll draw on that
experience to help event planners, property managers, and business owners think
through the security decisions that keep people and property safe.

## What you'll find here

- **Event-security planning** — how to scope coverage for concerts, festivals,
  high school events, and public gatherings of every size.
- **Property protection** — deterring theft and vandalism at construction sites,
  apartment complexes, and after-hours commercial properties.
- **Working with a security partner** — what to expect, what to ask, and how to
  get the most from a professional, accountable security team.
